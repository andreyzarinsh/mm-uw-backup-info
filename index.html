MM UW Backup rclone

A personal backup utility for the owner of the Maria Method / United Wisdom project.

This Google OAuth client is used by rclone on the owner's computer to upload and verify recovery archives in the owner's Google Drive. It is not a public file-sharing or hosting service.

Requested permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. The application uses this permission to create, read, update and remove the specific Google Drive files used with this application.

The backup workflow keeps four weekly and three monthly recovery points, with a 1 TB limit for its dedicated backup folder. Failed or unverified backups do not authorize deletion of the latest verified recovery point.
